## Verification and limits

Offline parser checks covered base64/hex decoding, nested text, LiveCodes query text, UTC timestamp handling, and omission of URL-shaped parameter keys. A mocked network entry point verified that static decoding performs no requests. JSON checks cover unique IDs and the published redaction policy.

The web-reader could not fetch some scanner pages, the Linuxiarz homepage timed out, and UseMod's recent-changes page exposed no usable changes in the checked response. Preflight scanner reads without the public page's HTMX context header were empty. These read failures and empty pages do not establish absence of activity. Public listings miss private, deleted and unsupported traces; original report reviews are limited to 22 selected records.
